    Our story

    Meraki — to leave a piece of yourself in what you make. Every panel is embroidered by hand, then cut for one woman at a time.

    Saima works with a small atelier in Lahore. Silks and raw cottons are sourced by the bolt, embroideries are drawn to order, and nothing is produced in volume — which is why some pieces exist only once.

    Privacy & Cookies

    Last updated 10 September 2026

    Meraki by Saima is a small womenswear label. This page explains, in plain terms, what this website collects, why, and what you can turn off.

    What you send us

    The only information you actively give this website is what you type into an inquiry. That is:

    • your name
    • your phone number
    • your email address
    • the U.S. state you are in
    • a preferred size, if you choose one
    • anything you write in the message box
    • which pieces you added to your inquiry

    We ask for these because an inquiry is the start of a conversation — Saima replies to you directly to talk through sizing, fabric, price and timing. There is no checkout on this site and no payment is taken here, so we never see card details.

    What the site stores in your browser

    Your inquiry list is kept in your own browser's local storage so it survives a refresh. So is a short identifier for an inquiry you are part-way through sending, which stops the same inquiry arriving twice if your connection drops, and your answer to the cookie question below. None of these are advertising cookies and none of them leave your device on their own.

    Analytics and advertising cookies

    If you accept, this site loads the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) that a browser visited a page, viewed a particular piece, or sent an inquiry, and it sets its own cookies to recognise that browser again. Meta uses this to measure our advertising and to show our pieces to people likely to be interested.

    We send Meta the page you are on, and for a product page the piece's name, its reference and its price. We do not send Meta your name, phone number, email address, your message, or anything else from an inquiry. When an inquiry is sent successfully we tell Meta that one happened, and nothing about who sent it or what it contained.

    If you decline, the Meta Pixel is never loaded and no Meta cookies are set. The site works the same either way.

    Changing your mind

    Your choice is remembered in your browser until you change it. Use Cookie preferences at the bottom of any page to switch. If you decline after previously accepting, we stop sending Meta anything from that moment — but we cannot undo information already sent, or remove cookies Meta has already set on your device. To clear those, use your browser's own settings for deleting cookies and site data.

    Who else handles this information

    We use a small number of services to run the site, and your information passes through them:

    • Vercel hosts the website and serves its pages.
    • Supabase stores inquiries in our database.
    • Meta receives site-activity information, but only if you accept.

    These providers keep their own server logs, which typically include IP addresses, as a normal part of running a website. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

    How long we keep inquiries

    Inquiries are kept for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of orders and commissions. We have not set a fixed deletion schedule, and we would rather say so than promise one we do not run. If you would like your inquiry removed, ask us and we will delete it.

    Your choices

    • Decline analytics and advertising cookies, at any time, using Cookie preferences.
    • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
    • Clear this site's stored data using your browser's settings, which also clears your inquiry list and your cookie choice.

    Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your information. Ask us and we will do our best to honour them.

    Children

    This site is meant for adults shopping for clothing. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

    Asking us about privacy

    Write to us with any question about this page or about information we hold, and we will answer.

    Changes to this page

    If we change how any of this works, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
